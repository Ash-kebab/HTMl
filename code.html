Classic Chochlate Chip Cookies


This is the best recipie for soft and chewey choclate chip cookies
They are incredibly easy to make and always a crowd pleaser!


Ingredients


  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup white sugra
  • 2 cups all - purpose flour
  • 2 cups choclate chips
  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
  2. Mix butter and sugar , then add flour and choclate chips
  3. Bake for 10 - 12 minutes until golden brown

Chef's note


Do not overbake the cookies It is highly recomendedto serve with a warm cup of milk.
